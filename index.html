FAQ

Questions, Answered.

What exactly do I get?

A professional contractor website, Google Search Ads setup, conversion tracking, lead capture, and 30 days of Google Ads campaign management.

Is the Google Ads budget included in the $799?

No. Your advertising spend is paid separately, directly to Google. The $799 covers Nexus Luma's work building and managing the system.

Why is the ad budget separate?

So you control exactly how much is spent on advertising. Your budget is billed by Google in your own Google Ads account.

Do you guarantee leads?

No. Results depend on market demand, competition, location, budget, and other factors. Nexus Luma builds and manages the system designed to generate and capture qualified inquiries.

What happens after the first 30 days?

You can manage the campaign yourself, or choose an optional ongoing Nexus Luma management plan. Nothing renews automatically.

Do I own my website?

Final website assets are delivered to you once all project invoices are settled, per our project terms.

What if I already have a website?

We'll evaluate whether your existing website can be used or whether a new conversion-focused build is the better fit.
